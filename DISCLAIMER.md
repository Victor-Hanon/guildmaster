## Your client, your files
Guildmaster contains no World of Warcraft client file (executable, archives, models, textures, music, sounds) and will never provide
one. You need your own 3.3.5a client. The data the server needs (maps, collisions, navigation, game tables) and the icons shown by
the app are prepared on your computer, from your client, and stay there.

## Private server, for you alone
The game server runs entirely on your computer. It can only be reached from this PC, and from your local network only if you enable
it (for the app on your phone). Do not expose it to the Internet. Guildmaster has no connection with the official servers and must
not be used with them: the launcher points your client's `realmlist.wtf` to your own server and can restore it.

## Alpha version
Guildmaster is in development. Expect defects, including lost progress. The launcher backs up your world before every update; please
make your own backups too ("Back up now" button). An update is offered, never forced.

## Hardcore mode
If you choose hardcore mode when creating your world, every death is permanent: your guild's adventurers as well as your own
characters, including when one of your characters is played by the AI while you are away. The mode is set when the world is created
or in Settings; switching back to normal mode does not bring back those who died in hardcore.
